# Skills

My personal instructions and recommendations for AI agents.

## Usage

Copy `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` into your project root, then adjust them to your needs.

I default to `AGENTS.md`.

Since Claude don't support `AGENTS.md` natively, `CLAUDE.md` just contains inside a hook pointing to `AGENTS.md`

I also highly recommend the skill set from [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) - one of the most valuable collections out there.

## Philosophy

The shorter, more specific, and more concrete the instructions, the better. The LLM will take care of the rest - just give it the information it actually needs.
